Export the List on This Page to a Clean Spreadsheet — One Click, Fully Local

PageScrape Export is a planned browser extension: while you are viewing a page — a marketplace search result, a job board, a property listing page — one click would detect the list or table on it and save a clean CSV or Excel file, with ready-made templates for e-commerce product lists, job postings, and property listings. Everything would run in your own browser: no cloud scraping service, no account, no data uploaded anywhere. It is not built yet. This page exists to answer one question first: do enough people copy web data into spreadsheets often enough to justify building it?

Why this shape

  • The tools people use today sit at two extremes: free scrapers that need you to build a sitemap of selectors by hand (or that miss rows on scroll-loaded pages), and cloud automation services starting around $50/month. Between them there is no simple one-time-purchase tool aimed at "the data on the page I am already looking at".
  • Data Miner charges $19.90/month and up for recipe-based extraction; webscraper.io's cloud plans start around $50/month. A $39 one-time local tool is the gap this page is testing — that proves a price gap exists, not that this product will sell.
  • Scope is deliberately narrow: only data visible on pages you open yourself. No background crawling, no bypassing logins or captchas, no bulk harvesting of sites. Sites that prohibit scraping in their terms are not template targets.

Planned capabilities (none of this exists yet)

  • One-click detection of lists and tables on the current page, with a preview before export
  • Export to CSV and Excel with clean column names and types
  • Vertical templates: e-commerce product lists, job postings, property/rental listings (registrants vote on which ships first)
  • Handles "load more" and scroll-loaded lists on the page you are viewing
  • Fully local processing — the page data never leaves your browser

This page promises nothing that is not written above. Features, if the product is built, will follow what registrants actually ask for.

Who this is for

  • E-commerce operators collecting competitor or supplier product lists
  • Recruiters and sales staff gathering job postings or lead lists from pages they browse
  • Anyone who regularly copies web tables into spreadsheets by hand
  • If you need scheduled, large-scale crawling of whole sites, this is not that tool — cloud services exist for that job

Questions

Can I use it now?
No. Nothing has been built; this page is the whole thing.

Does registering mean I have to buy it?
No. It only means "notify me if it launches". Decide when there is a real product to look at.

Why local-only?
Local processing means no server costs, no account, and your data stays on your machine. The trade-off is real: this will never do scheduled cloud crawling.

Is scraping websites allowed?
It depends on each site's terms, and this tool is scoped to exporting data from pages you are personally viewing. It will not bypass logins, captchas, or access controls, and template support will exclude sites that prohibit it. You are responsible for how you use exported data.
